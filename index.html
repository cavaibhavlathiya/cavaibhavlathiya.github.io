About

Finance that leadership can rely on

I am a Chartered Accountant (ICAI) and CPA (CPA Australia) with more than ten years in finance, accounting and audit. That includes over five years as Assistant Manager – Finance in trading, automotive and manufacturing businesses across the UAE, Oman and India.

My work covers the full reporting cycle: a controlled month-end close on Microsoft Dynamics 365, IFRS financial statements, and monthly management reports, dashboards and variance analysis for senior management. I support budgeting, forecasting and business performance reviews, and I manage the working capital and cash flow picture behind them.

An audit background shapes how I work. Every number in a management report should tie to a reconciled balance, and every balance should have a schedule that stands up to review.

Month-End Close · IFRS

Month-End Close and Balance Sheet Closing Framework on Dynamics 365

Problem

A multi-company automotive and trading group needs a close that delivers IFRS-compliant statements on time, with every balance sheet line supported by a schedule that management and auditors can review.

My Approach

  • Closing schedules for accruals, prepayments, IFRS 16 leases, inventory, sales and fixed assets, each tied to the GL
  • Adjusting journals posted in Dynamics 365 with supporting workings
  • Balance sheet, customer, supplier and intercompany reconciliations built into the close checklist

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365 (GL, AR, AP, Fixed Assets), Excel with Power Query

Outcome

Monthly P&L, balance sheet and cash flow statement prepared under IFRS, with a fully supported balance sheet and audit-ready closing files.

MIS · Power BI

Management Reporting Pack and Financial Dashboard

Problem

Senior management needs a consistent monthly view of revenue, margin, cost and performance against budget, without waiting for manual spreadsheets to be rebuilt each month.

My Approach

  • Standard monthly management report structure: P&L, key KPIs and actual vs budget
  • Dynamics 365 data extracted and modelled with Power Query and Power Pivot
  • Power BI dashboard for revenue, cost and profitability trends
  • Variance commentary that explains the drivers, not just the numbers

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365, Excel (Power Query, Power Pivot, VBA), Power BI

Outcome

Better financial reporting and visibility for management, with a repeatable monthly pack that supports business performance reviews.

Treasury · Working Capital

Cash Flow Forecasting and Working Capital Monitoring

Problem

Liquidity planning depends on a reliable view of expected receipts and payment commitments, and on early warning when receivables or supplier obligations start to drift.

My Approach

  • Daily cash position monitoring
  • Rolling cash flow forecast covering expected receipts and payment requirements
  • Receivables ageing and customer balance reviews, with follow-up alongside sales and credit teams
  • Supplier payment schedules aligned to the cash forecast

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365 (AR, AP, Cash and Bank), Excel

Outcome

Forward visibility of liquidity for operational and supplier payment planning, and closer control over receivables and working capital.

FP&A

Budgeting, Forecasting and Variance Analysis for a Diversified Trading Business

Problem

A diversified trading business in Oman needs annual budgets and periodic forecasts that management can use to track profitability and working capital across business lines.

My Approach

  • Prepared annual budgets and periodic forecasts
  • Monthly actual vs budget variance analysis covering revenue, costs and profitability
  • Supported financial performance reviews with management
  • Worked with ERP and finance teams on reporting requirements and process improvements

Tools

ERP reporting, Excel (Power Pivot, VBA)

Outcome

Structured budget and forecast cycle supporting management decisions, and stronger financial controls across departments.

Controls · VAT · Audit

Reconciliations, VAT Compliance and Audit Readiness (UAE and Oman)

Problem

Group businesses must keep intercompany balances agreed, meet VAT obligations in each jurisdiction and go into statutory audit with clean, supported balances.

My Approach

  • Regular balance sheet, customer, supplier and intercompany reconciliations
  • UAE VAT compliance checks within the monthly close
  • Managed Oman VAT compliance
  • Coordinated statutory audits with external auditors and tax consultants

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365, Excel, audit working paper structure

Outcome

Reconciled and supported balances, compliance with IFRS, UAE VAT and internal financial controls, and well-prepared audit files.
